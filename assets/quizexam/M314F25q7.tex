This quiz has six problems worth 10 points.
\begin{enumerate}
\item (4 points) Give examples of matrices for which the number of solutions to $A \mathbf{x} = \mathbf{b}$ is
	\begin{enumerate}
	\item 0 or 1, depending on $\mathbf{b}.$ Explain your reasoning.
	\vfill
	\item 0 or $\infty$,  depending on $\mathbf{b}.$ Explain your reasoning.
	\vfill
	\end{enumerate}
\item (4 points) Find a basis for each subspace below.
	\begin{enumerate}
	\item The subspace of $2 \times 2$ matrices consisting of all upper triangular matrices.
	\vfill
	\item The subspace of $\mathbb{R}^4$ consisting of all vectors orthogonal to $(1,1,0,0)$ and $(1,0,1,0).$	
	\vfill
	\end{enumerate}
\item (2 points) [Fill in the blank.] Suppose $A$ is an $m \times n.$
	\begin{enumerate}
	\item The vector $\textbf{d}$ is in the row space of $A$ when \underline{\hspace{1in}} has a solution. \\
	\item The row space of $A$ is a subspace of $\mathbb{R}^{\boxed{\phantom{n}}}.$
	\end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}
