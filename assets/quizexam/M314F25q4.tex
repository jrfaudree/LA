\begin{enumerate}
\item (4 points) For each augmented matrix below, state \textit{in words} what the next elimination step would be and write out the elimination matrix for that step. ( Your words can be something like, "Add row 1 to row 2." You do not have to perform the elimination step.)
	\begin{enumerate}
	\item $\bbm 1 &2& 3& 0\\ 0 &5 &-1& 7 \\ 0& -2& 0 &1\\ \ebm$
	\vfill
	\item $\bbm 1& 2 &3 &0\\ 0& 0 &-1& 7 \\ 0& 8& 4& 1\\ \ebm$
	\vfill
	\end{enumerate}
\item (2 points) The matrices $C$ and $D$ are written below. Of the two possible products, namely $CD$ or $DC$, only one is defined. \textbf{State} which one is defined and calculate the product.
 $$ C=\bbm 2 & \pi \\ -1 & 0 \ebm \hspace{.2in}D= \bbm 1&1\\-1&0\\1&2\\0&-1 \ebm$$
